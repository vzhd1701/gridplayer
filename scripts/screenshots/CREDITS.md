# Films in the screenshots

The screenshots show stills of Blender's open movies, cut into short clips and
cropped (by `get_clips.sh`). Each is under a Creative Commons Attribution
licence, which asks for this credit wherever the screenshots are shown.

| Film | By | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Big Buck Bunny](https://peach.blender.org/) (2008) | Blender Foundation | [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |
| [Spring](https://studio.blender.org/films/spring/) (2019) | Blender Animation Studio | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| [Coffee Run](https://studio.blender.org/films/coffee-run/) (2020) | Blender Studio | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| [Sprite Fright](https://studio.blender.org/films/sprite-fright/) (2021) | Blender Studio | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| [Hero](https://studio.blender.org/films/hero/) (2018) | Blender Studio | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| [Wing It!](https://studio.blender.org/films/wing-it/) (2023) | Blender Studio | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

Big Buck Bunny comes from [download.blender.org](https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/BBB/),
the others from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/).

Another film in its place needs a free licence that allows reuse (CC BY or
CC0, not NC or ND) and a row here.
